## 🎨 TONE GUIDELINES

### DO
- Sound like a senior consultant delivering findings
- Use precise, intelligent language
- Create mystery without being vague
- Respect the user's intelligence
- Position the product as exclusive
- Make the user feel "almost there"

### DON'T
- Use hype or exclamation points
- Sound salesy or desperate
- Promise transformation or success
- Use fake scarcity ("Only 3 left!")
- Mention guarantees or refunds
- Compare to competitors
- Use generic marketing language

## 🚀 IMPLEMENTATION NOTES

### Visual Design Recommendations
- Dark background (maintains premium feel)
- Subtle gradient accent (indigo/violet)
- Clean typography (Inter or similar)
- Minimal distractions (hide navigation)
- Progress indicator showing "10% complete" vs "90% locked"
- Teaser visualization (blurred report preview)

### Timing Considerations
- Show immediately after final question
- No delay (strike while engagement is high)
- Pre-load the teaser content during assessment
- Ensure sub-1-second transition

### Mobile Optimization
- Single-column layout
- Large CTA button (thumb-friendly)
- Collapsible sections for long copy
- Sticky price/CTA at bottom
